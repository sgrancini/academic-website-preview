Job Market Paper

Public Debt, iMPCs & Fiscal Policy Transmission

ECB Working Paper No. 3106

PaperECB WPSSRN

Using U.S. local projections with identified government-spending shocks, I study how public debt shapes fiscal policy transmission. The paper interprets smaller output and consumption multipliers at high household-sector Treasury positions through a heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian model.

Read abstract

Government bonds provide households with safe assets for saving and self-insurance. Higher household absorption of public debt raises the equilibrium real return, changes the distribution of liquid assets, and lowers intertemporal marginal propensities to consume, weakening private-consumption amplification.
