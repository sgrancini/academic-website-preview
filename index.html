I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at NOVA School of Business and Economics. My advisor is Pedro Brinca.

I am on the 2026–2027 Economics Job Market.

During my Ph.D., I was a Visiting Student Research Collaborator in the Economics Department at Princeton University, hosted by Gianluca Violante. I was also a Research PhD Trainee in the Directorate General Economics at the European Central Bank and participated in the IMF Fund Internship Program in the Fiscal Affairs Department.

Research Interests: Household Heterogeneity, Inequality, Wealth Distribution, Fiscal Policy, Monetary Policy, Production Networks, Labor Markets.
