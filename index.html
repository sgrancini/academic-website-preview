Job Market Paper

Public Debt, iMPCs & Fiscal Policy Transmission

ECB Working Paper No. 3106

PaperECB WPSSRN

Using U.S. local projections with identified government-spending shocks, I study how public debt shapes fiscal policy transmission. Output and consumption multipliers are smaller when the household-sector Treasury position is high relative to GDP. A heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian model relates this state dependence to government bonds’ role as safe assets for saving and self-insurance.
