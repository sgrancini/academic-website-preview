Job Market Paper

Public Debt, iMPCs & Fiscal Policy Transmission

ECB Working Paper No. 3106

PaperECB WPSSRN

Using U.S. local projections with identified government-spending shocks, I find that output and consumption multipliers are smaller when the household-sector Treasury position is high relative to GDP. To interpret this state dependence, I develop a heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian model in which government bonds provide households with safe assets for saving and self-insurance.

Selected presentations: Princeton; ECB; IMF; European Economic Association; CEPR Economics Job Market Bootcamp.

Working Papers

Welfare Analysis of Income-Stabilization Policies in a HANK Model with Unemployment Risk

with Marcos Poplawski-Ribeiro and Danila Smirnov

IMF Working Paper No. 2026/076

PaperIMF WP

Understanding how policies can stabilize household welfare during recessions requires a framework with household heterogeneity, unemployment risk, and general-equilibrium labor-market dynamics. We compare unemployment-insurance generosity, targeted transfers to hand-to-mouth households, and universal transfers in a HANK model with search-and-matching frictions.
